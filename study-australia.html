What to know
Studying in Australia
Seven of Australia's universities rank in the global top 100, and the visa process is among the most transparent.

Degree structure

Bachelor's degrees generally take three years (four with honours), master's one to two years. Many programmes include industry placements or work-integrated learning.

Fees & living costs

International tuition typically ranges from AUD 22,000 to AUD 45,000 per year depending on level and discipline. Living costs are concentrated in Sydney and Melbourne.

Entry requirements

You'll need an accepted offer, proof of English (IELTS, TOEFL or PTE), academic transcripts and Genuine Student documentation.

Student visa (500)

The Subclass 500 process is online. You need a Confirmation of Enrolment, financial capacity evidence, health cover (OSHC) and a genuine temporary entrant statement.

Work rights

Students can work up to 48 hours per fortnight during term and unlimited hours during breaks. The Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485) gives two to four years of post-study work rights.

Strong fields

Mining and resources engineering, environmental science, agriculture, healthcare, accounting and information technology.
