How essay support works
Every application needs a different essay. Here is how we approach it.

You write the raw material

The essay has to sound like you. We start with a structured interview about your experiences, motivations and goals — not a template.

We help you structure it

Turning a rough set of experiences into a coherent narrative: what to include, what to cut, and how to connect your past to the programme you're applying to.

Multiple revisions

Essays improve through editing. We work through drafts with you rather than handing you a finished document you didn't write.

Final review

Grammar, consistency, tone, and a check that you have actually answered the prompt the university asked.

Programme-specific

Each university and programme has its own prompt and priorities. The essay is tailored, not recycled across applications.

Feedback you can reach

Questions between drafts get answered — no ticket queue, no waiting a week for a reply.
