Our mission
Honest guidance. Real outcomes.
The admissions consulting industry has a commission problem. Universities pay agents to send students — creating a conflict of interest where your best outcome isn't always the priority. ACS was built from day one to change that.

No Commission. Ever.

We don't accept referral fees or commissions from any university, anywhere. Our only incentive is your success — not a payout tied to where you enrol.

Radical Transparency

We tell you the truth about your profile — including honest assessments of your chances and realistic alternatives, even when it's not what you want to hear.

Student-First, From Day One

Because we're a young, focused team, every student gets direct access to senior counsellors — not a call centre. You're not a ticket number to us.
