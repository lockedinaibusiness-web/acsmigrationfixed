Why India
A rising global education hub
India is home to IITs, IIMs, AIIMS, and hundreds of NAAC/NBA-accredited universities — offering degrees respected across the world, at a fraction of the cost of studying abroad.

Affordable, Quality Education

Tuition and living costs are significantly lower than the US, UK, or Australia — without compromising on faculty quality or infrastructure.

Globally Recognized Degrees

Degrees from UGC-recognized universities are accepted for higher studies and employment across most countries, including for further study abroad.

English-Medium Instruction

Most professional courses — engineering, medicine, management — are taught entirely in English, easing the transition for international students.

Strong Medical & Engineering Base

India trains more doctors and engineers annually than most countries, backed by institutions like AIIMS, IITs, and NITs.

Simple Visa Process for Neighbours

Students from Nepal and Bhutan can study in India with minimal visa formalities, while other international students use the Student Visa route.

Culturally Familiar

Shared culture, cuisine, and — in many cases — language make the transition easier for students from South Asia compared to studying in the West.
