What to know
Studying in Japan
A distinctive system with generous government scholarships and clear language pathways.

Degree structure

Bachelor's degrees take four years, master's two. Undergraduate entry usually requires 12 years of schooling; graduate entry needs 16 years or equivalent.

Language pathways

Most degree programmes are taught in Japanese (N2–N1 level). English-taught programmes exist at universities including Tokyo, Waseda and Tohoku. Language schools prepare you either way.

Scholarships

The MEXT (Japanese Government) scholarship covers tuition, a monthly stipend and airfare. JASSO and a wide range of university-specific scholarships also exist.

Student visa

A Certificate of Eligibility sponsored by your school comes first, then the visa itself. Financial proof and admission confirmation are required.

Work rights

Students need a work permit (Permission to Engage in Activity Other Than That Permitted) and can work up to 28 hours per week during term.

Strong fields

Robotics, engineering, computer science, animation and design, materials science and Japanese language studies.
