Why Canada
Why Indian students love Canada

Affordable Tuition

Canada offers world-class education at significantly lower tuition costs than the USA or UK — especially at public universities.

PR-Friendly Pathways

Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) + Express Entry — many graduates gain Canadian Permanent Residency within 2–3 years.

Work While Studying

Study permit holders can work 20 hours per week off-campus during terms — and full-time during scheduled breaks.

Large Indian Community

One of the largest Indian diaspora communities — cities like Toronto, Vancouver, and Brampton feel instantly familiar.

Top-Ranked Universities

University of Toronto, UBC, McGill, Waterloo — all globally recognised for research and industry connections.

Safe & Multicultural

Consistently ranked among the world's safest and most tolerant countries — ideal for international students.
